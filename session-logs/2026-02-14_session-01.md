### 3. User's Vision Articulated

The user described their full vision:
- **Problem**: Keyboard/trackpad is limiting. As an architect who loves drawing, wants to work spatially.
- **Solution**: Camera watches workspace + projector projects onto it + Claude is the brain
- **Interaction**: Draw on paper/whiteboard/table. Annotate with stickies. System reads everything.
- **OpenCV app**: Projects buttons for interaction, can show small "windows" of content on the surface
- **Audio**: Always listening, can hear voice commands
- **Two use cases**: (a) Web design — sketch and co-design (b) Arduino/physical computing — guided builds
- **Eventual goal**: Tie into Folk Computer for full system
